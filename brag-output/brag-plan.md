# Brag Plan: Password Manager

## Planning rubric
1. A web vault and Chrome/Edge extension for storing encrypted credentials, generating passwords, and explicitly filling matching login forms.
2. Strongest claim: Your passwords. Off your mind.
3. Visual hook: the terracotta lock and a field of mental password tabs clearing away.
4. Real UI: the bundled vault-desktop.png and extension-vault.png, already populated with fictional sample accounts.
5. Shortest satisfying film: 20 seconds.
6. Tone: polished; a warm editorial product film, calm and precise.
7. Audio: steady clean music, a reveal accent and two quiet interaction clicks.
8. Caption: Keep the passwords. Lose the mental tabs. Password Manager brings your vault, locally generated passwords, and browser filling together.
9. Flow: open the vault -> generate a password -> choose a matching account and fill a sign-in form. Demonstration never submits.

## Identity
Cream #fbfaf7; ink #25263d; terracotta #c45b48; lavender #efedf6; navy #18214d. Plus Jakarta Sans display, Inter body, both copied from project WOFF2 files. Product lock copied from icon.svg.

## Angle and hook
Replace password anxiety with a single calmer home. First 3 seconds: three password reminders leave as the exact headline Your passwords. Off your mind. arrives. Large, settled typography.

4. Off your mind, 16-20s (4s). Navy field; product lock, Password Manager and exact copy Keep the passwords. Lose the mental tabs. Cream CTA Create your vault. Hold at least 2s. Soft landing and music fade.
