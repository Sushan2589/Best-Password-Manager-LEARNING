# Password Manager launch video

Open watch.html to play, or open brag.mp4 directly.

- brag.mp4: 20-second 1920 × 1080 launch film at 30 fps, with music and interface sounds.
- brag.jpg: selected settled vault-and-generator frame, also baked into frame zero.
- share-copy.txt: ready-to-post caption with required music attribution.
- CREDITS.txt: music, sound, and font credits.
- composition/: editable, self-contained HyperFrames source and local assets.
- brag-plan.md and composition-brief.md: storyboard, creative direction, source grounding.
- check-report.json: full validation report, including text contrast coverage.

The vault and extension screenshots are project sample assets with fictional accounts. Generator and field-suggestion interactions are source-informed demo recreations. No form is submitted.

To preview or render again, run npm run dev or npm run render -- --quality delivery --fps 30 --output ../brag.mp4 from composition/. Scripts pin HyperFrames 0.8.214. Re-rendering does not automatically bake the poster into frame zero; retain the separate brag.jpg for custom thumbnails.

Music is CC BY 4.0: retain its attribution when sharing. Local fonts and their licenses are included.
