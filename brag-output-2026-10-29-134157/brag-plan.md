# Password Manager — a little less to remember

1. App: an encrypted web vault with a password generator and browser extension.
2. Strongest copy: “Your passwords. Off your mind.”
3. Hook: password reminders clear away as the terracotta headline lands.
4. Actual UI: project-provided vault and extension screenshots, plus a source-informed interactive generator and inline account picker.
5. Duration: 20 seconds, 1920 × 1080 landscape, 30 fps.
6. Tone: polished; warm, restrained product demonstration.
7. Audio: Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol.12 by Sascha Ende, with sparse Kenney clicks and soft reveal accents. No narration.
8. Caption: Keep the passwords. Lose the mental tabs. A web vault, locally generated passwords, and matching logins beside the field.
9. Flow: open vault → generate a password → choose a matching account beside a login field → review filled credentials.

LOOK: cream #fbfaf7 ground, ink #25263d, terracotta #c45b48 accents, lavender #efedf6 stage, navy #18214d outro; Plus Jakarta Sans 650 display and Inter body; 110px frame margins, 74–116px headlines, 28px supporting text, 2px interface borders.

## Storyboard

- 0–3s, Clear your head. Real product headline, lock mark, three departing password reminders. Headline settles by 0.7s and holds. Warm impact at 0.1s. Quick leftward departure.
- 3–10s, Give your logins a home. Actual vault screenshot beside a three-line title. Generator opens at 5.3s. Cursor presses Generate at 6.56s; a fictional 20-character password and Very strong label appear, holding through 9.75s. One click accompanies the press.
- 10–16s, Right beside the field. Actual extension screenshot and fictional sign-in form. Cursor focuses Email at 11.46s; matching account appears underneath. Cursor selects that account at 13.11s, a strong musical cue. Both fields fill; the sign-in button remains untouched. Result holds through 15.75s. Two quiet clicks follow these actions.
- 16–20s, Lose the mental tabs. Navy end card, product name, exact footer tagline and Create your vault. Main line lands by 16.55s; CTA holds for over 2s. Music fades over the last 1.2s.
